# Missouri Pretrial and Trial Preparation

Turn a set Missouri civil trial date into a working plan: everything due before trial, everything needed in the courtroom, and every objection preserved.

## When to use
- A trial or pretrial conference date has been set in a Missouri circuit or associate circuit case.
- The user asks about witness/exhibit lists, subpoenas, motions in limine, or jury waiver.
- Not for: criminal trial prep - use `mo-criminal-case-roadmap`; courtroom conduct on the day - use `mo-pro-se-courtroom-procedure`; jury instruction drafting - use `mo-jury-instructions-mai`.

## Gather first
- The trial setting order and any pretrial/scheduling order (exact deadlines for lists, exhibits, motions, instructions).
- The live claims and defenses, and the elements the user must prove (or rebut).
- The evidence inventory: witnesses, documents, recordings, depositions, discovery responses.

## Workflow
1. Calendar every deadline in the trial setting and pretrial orders; local rules and the judge's orders control over defaults. Rule 62 authorizes pretrial conferences to simplify issues, amend pleadings, obtain admissions/stipulations, and limit expert witnesses.
2. Jury or bench decision: the right to a jury exists for actions at law (Mo. Const. art. I, sec. 22(a)); equitable claims are tried to the court. Rule 69.01 lists ways a jury is waived (e.g., failing to appear, written or oral consent, proceeding to bench trial without objection - verify text). In associate circuit cases, check whether a written jury demand is required by statute or local rule. If bench trial, consider requesting findings under Rule 73.01 BEFORE evidence is introduced.
3. Build an elements-to-proof chart: each element -> witness/exhibit -> foundation -> anticipated objection.
4. Witnesses: list each, purpose, expected testimony, and how they will get to court. Serve subpoenas (Rule 57.09) early, with any required fees; confirm service. Plan for unavailable witnesses via deposition (use at trial governed by Rule 57.07).
5. Exhibits: pre-mark, make copies (court, opposing party, witness, self). Use business records affidavits (RSMo 490.692) where possible, served on the other side before trial within the statute's notice period - verify. Seek stipulations to authenticity.
6. Motions in limine: file to exclude specific prejudicial or inadmissible evidence (hearsay, prior bad acts, insurance, settlement talks). A ruling in limine is interlocutory and preserves nothing; you must object (or make an offer of proof) at trial when the evidence comes up.
7. Jury trials: prepare voir dire questions, cause challenges, and peremptory strategy (number of civil peremptory strikes is statutory - verify in RSMo 494.480). Draft proposed instructions per MAI.
8. Prepare an opening statement outline, direct and cross outlines, and a closing tied to the verdict director or the elements.
9. Directed verdict planning: move for directed verdict at the close of the opponent's evidence and again at the close of all evidence - required to later seek JNOV (Rule 72.01).
10. Build the trial notebook and a day-of checklist.

## Output
- Pretrial deadline table: Item | Source (order/rule) | Due date | Status.
- Elements-to-proof chart.
- Witness list and exhibit list templates (No. | Description | Sponsoring witness | Foundation | Offered | Admitted).
- Motion in limine outline: Caption; Evidence at issue; Rule/authority; Prejudice; Requested order.
- Trial notebook tabs: Pleadings, Orders, Elements chart, Witness outlines, Exhibits, Motions in limine and rulings, Instructions, Opening/Closing, Legal authorities.

## Pitfalls
- Missing an exhibit/witness disclosure deadline - testimony or exhibits may be excluded.
- Relying on a granted or denied motion in limine without objecting at trial.
- Waiving jury by not appearing or by proceeding without objection.
- Forgetting to request findings in a bench trial before evidence starts.
- Not moving for directed verdict at the close of all evidence (forfeits JNOV).
- Subpoenas served late or without fees; witnesses who do not show.
- No offer of proof when evidence is excluded.

## Verify before relying
- Pull verbatim Rules 57.07, 57.09, 62, 69.01, 72.01, 73.01 and RSMo 490.692, 494.480 via `statute-lookup`.
- Read the judge's trial order and local rules; they override defaults.
- Recompute all disclosure deadlines from the trial date per the order.
- Legal information, not legal advice; consider limited-scope counsel for trial.
